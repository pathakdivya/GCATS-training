What am I holding?

You're holding a gamepad which is a handheld device with two thumbsticks and several buttons. Each part sends its own signal to the computer, so that the computer can read exactly how far a stick is tilted or which buttons are pressed. You can press any button or move either stick to see the controller respond in the illustration on the right.

Important components:

  • Thumbsticks — two small joysticks that you can tilt 360°.
  • Shoulder buttons — sitting along the top edge under your index fingers.

How to hold it?

In the upcoming page you will see two ways you can hold the controller for our experiment. You can choose between the two options based on your comfort. The choice you make will be used throughout the remaining stages.
